In a world full of stories and meaning, the city of Jakarta presents a fascinating tale. Bhaskara, with its red thread running through the history of Batavia, Sunda Kelapa, and Metropolitan Jakarta, aims to transport viewers across dimensions of space and time. The visual presentation, accompanied by harmonious music, displays connections from the past to the present. A blends of Jakarta's arts and culture with the ethnic diversity of Indonesia.

Moodboard & Reference

This project draws inspiration from Malaysia’s multicultural identity, incorporating influences from Malay, Chinese, and Indian cultures. Architectural elements, patterns, and visual motifs from each culture were integrated to create visuals that reflect Malaysia’s diversity and unity. The moodboard explores a balance of warm and cool color palettes, representing the contrast and harmony between the different cultural influences within the project.

3D assets

The project integrates 3D modelling to create buildings inspired by the three major cultures of Malaysia, Malay, Chinese, and Indian. Combining their architectural elements into one building. The project also includes modelling and animating the main villain, inspired by the Rafflesia flowercreate a distinctive character design.

My Contribution and Role

My role in this project mainly involved being the storyboard artist, 3D modeler, editor, animator, and compositor. Even though the five of us had our own individual roles, we still collaborated and helped each other throughout the project. After the rough storyboard sketches were completed, I refined and colored them to create the final proper storyboard.

Part of the main building and the main villain were modeled and animated by me alongside another team member. While each of us worked on our own sections for editing and animation, the compositing process was done together as a team, with me mainly handling the combining and final arrangement behind the screen.
