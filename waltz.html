The story revolves around a dancer who goes through the four seasons: Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter. Each transition between seasons serves as a metaphor for the passage of time, symbolizing growth, change, and the journey toward the future. This journey represents the arrival of a new year: 2026.

Moodboard & Reference

This project draws inspiration from the elegance and expressive movements of ballet dance. By utilizing the grace, fluidity, and variety of ballet choreography, the project aims to capture the essence of the four different seasons around the world. Each season is portrayed through unique movements, emotions, and visual atmospheres that symbolize change, growth, and the passage of time. Representing the arrival of the new year 2026, the performance blends artistic storytelling with dynamic motion to create meaningful visual experience.

Animation

Here are the 3D models after being animated and paired with the motion capture dance movements. Each choreography is carefully designed to represent a different season, emphasizing their unique characteristics, emotions, and atmosphere through movement and expression. The flowing and elegant ballet-inspired motions help bring the models to life, allowing every performance to visually portray the transition between seasons. From soft and graceful movements symbolizing spring, energetic and lively motions for summer, calm and flowing gestures for autumn, to delicate and dramatic performances representing winter, each dance creates its own distinct identity.
